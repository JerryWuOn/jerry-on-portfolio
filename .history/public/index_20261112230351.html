Monograph Essay // 01

From the tactile weight of Fifth Avenue vitrines to sub-second headless responsive canvases.

Digital merchandising is rarely treated as an architectural practice; it is frequently reduced to mechanical grid management and static SKU population. My practice was forged in the flagship windows of Rue Saint-Honoré and Madison Avenue, where merchandising is an emotional choreography—a physical modulation of drapery, distance, specular highlight, and quiet intrigue.

Transitioning to the digital sphere in 2016, I identified a systemic fracture: luxury online flagships were behaving like discount warehouse search indexes, stripping collections of their narrative silhouette. Over the past decade, I have pioneered a hybrid methodology that fuses haute-couture editorial staging with programmatic e-commerce science.

By translating physical garment weight, tactile textile behavior, and bespoke seasonal pacing into automated category ranking algorithms, complete-the-look hotspotting, and zero-latency shopping experiences, my studio enables heritage houses to achieve unprecedented AOV performance without ever compromising brand reverence.
